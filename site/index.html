NEXUS

Your thinking, connected.

An offline-first notes app for Android. Write in Markdown, link notes with [[wiki-links]], explore them as a graph, search everything on the device, and sync across your devices when you're online.

Download the Android app Source code and documentation
Installing
  1. Open the latest release and download nexus-x.y.z.apk.
  2. Open the file; Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser.
  3. Tap Continue offline to start, or create an account to sync.
A portfolio project. Notes stay on your device unless you sign in to sync.
